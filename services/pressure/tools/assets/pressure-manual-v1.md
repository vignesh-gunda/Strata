# PRESSURE — Player's Manual

*Version 1.0. Recovered from a shared drive backup. Section 4 was revised in a later release; this copy predates that revision.*

---

## 1. Overview

Pressure is a card game for 2 to 4 players. Everyone pushes a shared pressure gauge upward. Whoever pushes it over the limit is out. The last player standing wins.

## 2. Setup

1. Shuffle the deck.
2. Deal 4 cards to each player, one at a time, starting with the first player.
3. Place the remaining cards face down as the draw pile.
4. Set the pressure gauge to 0.

## 3. Taking a turn

On your turn you get one action. Choose one:

- **Play** a card from your hand.
- **Draw** the top card of the draw pile. You may hold up to 7 cards.
- **Pass**, but only if you cannot play or draw.

Play then moves to the next player.

## 4. The cards

| Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| Gear 1 | Add 1 to the pressure gauge. |
| Gear 2 | Add 2 to the pressure gauge. |
| Gear 3 | Add 3 to the pressure gauge. |
| Vent | Lower the pressure gauge by 3. It never goes below 0. |
| Reverse | Reverse the direction of play. |
| Double | The next player takes 2 actions on their turn. |
| Jam | Jam the next player. Their next card is wasted: it is discarded and does nothing. |
| Scan | Look at the top 3 cards of the draw pile. |

## 5. Overflow

The gauge's limit is 12. If a card you play brings the pressure to 12 or more, you **overflow**:

- You are out of the game.
- Your hand is discarded.
- The gauge resets to 0.
- Play moves to the next player.

## 6. Winning

When only one player remains, that player wins.
